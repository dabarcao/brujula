- (decision by coach) Trust/anonymity cluster is locked as shared, direction-agnostic requirements for both candidate directions, not a differentiator
- (decision by coach) Direction A - Quiet Compass: restrained, therapeutic-coaching register. Warm paper neutral background, one muted terracotta/clay accent, generous whitespace, humanist serif headings, soft-gradient low-saturation radar, minimal motion (fade not draw-in), compass motif used sparingly and abstractly, BetterUp/Headspace/Notion-leaning
- (decision by coach) Direction B - Confident Compass: warmer, brand-forward, still non-punitive. Slightly richer warm-neutral background, one confident indigo-or-forest accent used boldly on cards and CTAs, color-blocked dashboard cards, bolder compass-rose as a recurring graphic identity element, axis-by-axis chart draw-in animation, gentle celebratory micro-motion in the wizard only, Lattice/Culture-Amp-leaning consumer polish
- (decision by coach) Both directions share identical component structure/tokens contract (packages/ui primitives) - only token values, illustration/motion intensity, and iconographic boldness differ between them
- (insight by coach) Synthesis: the trust/anonymity signaling ideas (persistent micro-copy, aggregate-only chrome, pooled-response reassurance, identifiability nudge, pause/resume) are not a style choice - they are the product's core value proposition made visible, and must ship identically regardless of which visual direction wins. The real fork is emotional register: Direction A (Quiet Compass) treats feedback as private reflection; Direction B (Confident Compass) treats it as an energizing team ritual. Both reject red/green scoring and both use the compass motif, at different intensities, tying visual identity to the product name itself.
- (decision by user) Final choice: Direction B - Brújula Segura (Confident Compass) - indigo/coral color-blocked, Sora/Inter type, bolder radius/shadow, axis-drawn radar motion, energizing team-ritual register
